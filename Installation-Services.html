Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our services

How do I schedule a service appointment?

You can schedule a service appointment by calling our customer service hotline, using our mobile app, or filling out the contact form on our website. Our team will get back to you within 24 hours to confirm your appointment.

What is included in the maintenance advice service?

Our maintenance advice service includes a comprehensive vehicle inspection, personalized maintenance schedule, recommendations for optimal performance, and tips for extending your vehicle's lifespan. We also provide guidance on DIY maintenance tasks you can perform at home.

Do you offer services for electric vehicles?

Yes, we specialize in electric vehicle services. Our EV guidance includes charging solutions, battery maintenance, software updates, and performance optimization. Our technicians are trained to work with all major EV brands and models.

What is your service warranty policy?

We offer different warranty periods based on your service plan. Basic plans include a 7-day warranty, Pro plans include a 30-day warranty, and Premium plans include a 90-day warranty. Our warranty covers any issues related to the service provided.

Can you help with custom vehicle builds?

Absolutely! Our custom build service is one of our specialties. We work closely with you to understand your vision and bring it to life. From performance enhancements to aesthetic modifications, our team has the expertise to handle custom projects of all scales.
